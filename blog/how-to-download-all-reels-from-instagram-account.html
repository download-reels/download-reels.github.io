How to Download All Reels from an Instagram Account or YouTube Channel (Bulk 100-Reel ZIP Export)

Content creators and video editors often need to backup their video catalog, repurpose vertical shorts across platforms, or create compilation videos. Here is how modern web architecture enables bulk downloading up to 100 reels with pagination and ZIP compression.

1. The Problem with Single-Link Downloaders

Traditional video downloaders force users into an agonizing, repetitive loop: copy link #1, switch tabs, paste, click download, wait 10 seconds, and repeat for 50 different videos. For a creator with hundreds of reels, archiving content takes hours.

Download Reels solves this with a native Channel & Profile Reels Browser:

  • Visual 9:16 Gallery: Browse an entire account's feed with thumbnails and captions.
  • Max 100 Selection Ceiling: Select up to 100 reels at once with a single click.
  • Infinite Pagination: Load subsequent pages of 100 reels using native continuation tokens.
  • Client-Side ZIP Packaging: Bundle all selected MP4s into a single compressed `.zip` file on your device.

2. Step-by-Step: How to Bulk Download an Account's Reels

Step 1: Switch to "Channel & Profile Reels" Mode

On the homepage, click the Channel & Profile Reels pill in the top switcher bar.

Step 2: Paste Creator Profile or YouTube Channel Link

Enter an Instagram profile URL (e.g. https://www.instagram.com/light_of_quranic_knowledge/) or YouTube channel link (e.g. https://www.youtube.com/@TED/shorts) and click Browse Reels 🔍.

Step 3: Select Reels and Download

Click individual reel cards to select, or click Select All (Max 100). Then hit Download Selected or Download ZIP to save all files immediately.

3. Under the Hood: How Channel Pagination Works

Modern social media feeds use cursor-based pagination rather than traditional page numbers:

// YouTube Channel Shorts browse request

POST https://www.youtube.com/youtubei/v1/browse

{

"context": { "client": { "clientName": "WEB", "clientVersion": "2.20241025.01.00" } },

"continuation": "4qmFsgKxDBIYVUNBdVVVblQ..."

}

When you click Load Next 100 Reels, our Cloudflare edge worker resolves the continuation cursor, extracts the next set of progressive MP4 streams from platform CDNs, and appends them to your live browser view without any page reload.

4. Client-Side ZIP Packaging: Zero Server Storage

Unlike commercial services that store gigabytes of user videos on expensive AWS S3 buckets (and charge monthly subscriptions), Download Reels uses client-side Web Streams and JSZip.

When you download 50 reels as a ZIP, each video chunk streams directly from platform CDNs through a transparent CORS proxy straight into browser memory, where JSZip compresses them locally into a single `.zip` archive. Your files never touch a centralized storage database, guaranteeing 100% privacy and zero subscription fees.
