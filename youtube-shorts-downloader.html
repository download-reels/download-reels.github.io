Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about downloading reels and videos online.

Why do 1080p and 720p YouTube Shorts downloads have full audio here?

YouTube serves 1080p and 720p streams as separate video and audio tracks (DASH). Download Reels automatically merges the HD video track and HQ audio track inside your browser using FFmpeg WebAssembly (-c copy) with zero quality loss.

Can I convert YouTube videos and shorts to 320kbps MP3?

Yes. When you paste any YouTube video or short, select the "MP3 Audio (320 kbps Ultra HQ)" pill or click "Convert Format" to encode the stream directly in your browser using our client-side FFmpeg WebAssembly engine.

How does the client-side FFmpeg conversion work?

Download Reels loads a WebAssembly compilation of FFmpeg directly inside your web browser. This processes, transcodes, trims, and converts video/audio on your own computer CPU without uploading your files to any remote server, guaranteeing 100% privacy and zero server queues.

How do I download Instagram Reels without a watermark?

Copy the reel link directly from the Instagram mobile app or desktop browser, paste it into the URL bar above, and click Download. You will receive the original high-resolution MP4 video file with no added stamps or watermarks.

Can I download TikTok videos without the TikTok watermark?

Yes! Download Reels connects directly to clean CDN streams via TikWM API, extracting the pure 1080p/720p HD MP4 video with no floating TikTok logo or username stamps.

How do I save YouTube Shorts in 1080p MP4 format?

Paste any YouTube Shorts or standard YouTube URL into Download Reels. Our client stream resolver instantly extracts the progressive MP4 streams and audio tracks for immediate download.

Is Download Reels completely free and safe to use?

Yes, 100% free and open-source. There is no software to install, no sign-up or registration required, and no tracking cookies stored on your device.

How do I download reels on iPhone or Android mobile devices?

You can either open download-reels.github.io in your mobile Safari/Chrome browser, or generate a Mobile QR code on your computer screen using the QR button and scan it with your phone camera to download directly.
